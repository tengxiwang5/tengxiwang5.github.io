I am a PhD student at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou), advised by Prof. Wufan Zhao in the AI4City group.

My research combines computer vision, multimodal learning, and agentic workflows for remote sensing. I am particularly interested in mapping 2D land cover transitions, estimating 3D height changes, and generating descriptions of how urban environments change.

ISPRS JPRS 2026

Towards comprehensive multi-task land cover change detection leveraging vision-language model and LLM-driven agents

Tengxi Wang, Yiru Wang, Shuai Zhang, Yuxuan Liang, Wufan Zhao*

PaperCodeDataset

GaiaAgent combines a vision-language model with an agent workflow to detect and analyze 2D land cover changes and 3D height changes using the UrbanChange benchmark.

IEEE TGRS 2026

DSTI-Net: A Dynamic Spatial-Temporal Interaction Network with Semantic Guidance for 2-D and 3-D Change Detection

Tengxi Wang, Shuai Zhang, Mengmeng Li, Wufan Zhao*

Paper

DSTI-Net links changes in 2-D land cover with changes in 3-D structure by learning how semantic and geometric features interact across observation times.

Applied Energy 2027

Electrochemical-guided time-frequency transformer for battery state-of-health estimation

Hongyu Sun, Peidong Zheng, Junying Wei, Tengxi Wang, Qiang Liu

Paper

EG-Former uses electrochemical indicators to identify degradation-sensitive regions and combines graph and time-frequency features to estimate battery state of health.

Preprint 2026

Any2Building: Unifying Point Clouds and Images for Building Reconstruction via Shared Spatial Queries

Hongye Hou, Zhuoxiao Li, Liu Xiangyu, Tengxi Wang, Jing Ou, Dongli Wu, Shuai Zhang, Tongyan Hua, Wufan Zhao

arXiv

Any2Building reconstructs structured 3-D buildings at LoD2 from partial point clouds, overhead images, or both, using a shared set of spatial queries.
